New in NYC
IEOR 4570 Project 2 · Twyla, Alisa, Ungu

A research desk for settling into New York.

Ask one question. A team of AI agents looks things up in the city's own records and writes you a report where every fact links to its source.

What it explores

How NYC neighborhoods and buildings compare on housing records (violations, tenant complaints, rats, bedbugs), which free events and giveaways are listed nearby, and what it costs to furnish a room.

Who it is for

Anyone choosing where to live or settling in: new arrivals, students deciding where to stay after graduating, anyone moving neighborhoods.

How to use it

Type a question or pick an example. If it is too vague, the Lead asks you one question back. Open Research trail on any answer to see which agents ran and every tool call they made. It shows evidence and what the data cannot tell you; it does not pick for you.

The team

  • Lead plans, may ask you a question
  • Housing NYC housing records
  • Events free events and giveaways
  • Shopping furniture that fits
  • Writer the cited report

Try a question

Answers take 10 to 90 seconds because the researchers work live.

Data: NYC Open Data (HPD violations, complaints, rodent inspections, bedbug reports, registrations), NYC Parks events, NYC for FREE, Open-Meteo and Amazon search results. Wait times and curb scores are our own estimates, not data.
